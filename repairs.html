Repairs & Maintenance

Keep your industrial floors performing like new. Casian IRF repairs damaged surfaces, seals cracks and carries out planned maintenance to extend floor life, with programmes tailored to minimise downtime and protect your investment.

Why regular maintenance matters

Avoids full replacements

Small repairs now prevent a much bigger job later.

Removes trip hazards

Cracks, holes and lifting coatings are a health and safety risk.

Keeps chemical protection

Damaged coatings let chemicals and water attack the concrete.

Extends service life

A maintained floor lasts far longer than one left to fail.

Keeps you running

Planned work fits around your operations instead of emergencies.

Looks professional

A clean, intact floor reflects well on your site at audits and visits.

Repair before it spreads

Floor damage rarely stays small. A crack lets water and chemicals into the slab, edges break away under wheels, and before long a patch repair has become a full replacement. Catching problems early is almost always the cheapest option.

We repair resin floors and bare concrete alike, using fast-curing materials where you can’t afford to close an area for long. If a floor is past repair, we’ll tell you honestly and quote for the right replacement system.

Frequently asked questions

Can you repair a floor someone else installed?

Yes. We repair and refurbish existing resin and concrete floors regardless of who laid them, matching the system and colour as closely as possible.

How quickly can a repaired area be used again?

Many repair materials cure in a matter of hours, and rapid-cure systems can have an area back in use the same day. We’ll plan the materials around how long you can close the area.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We can set up scheduled inspections and maintenance visits so small issues are fixed before they disrupt your operations.
