About Casian IRF

Casian IRF (Casian Industrial Resin Flooring) is a specialist contractor for high-performance epoxy, polyurethane and MMA flooring. We combine thorough surface preparation with premium materials to deliver seamless, chemical-resistant floors built to last.

Based in Ashbourne, Co. Meath, we work on projects of any scale across Ireland, from manufacturing plants and warehouses to food processing facilities, commercial kitchens and car parks. Our clients include Cullens Fruit & Vegetables, K&K Produce and Bid Farm Ltd.

  • Site survey and system recommendation before any work starts
  • Full surface preparation, so coatings bond and last
  • Work scheduled around your operations to minimise downtime
  • Adhesion testing and final inspection at handover
  • Fully insured for commercial and industrial sites

How We Work

1

Consultation & quote

We assess your site and recommend the right resin system for your traffic, chemical exposure and budget.

2

Surface preparation

We remove contaminants, repair damage and profile the concrete so the coating bonds properly.

3

Professional application

Primer, build coats and topcoats are applied by our own crew, with anti-slip aggregate where needed.

4

Inspection & handover

After curing we carry out adhesion tests and a final inspection before handing the floor back.
